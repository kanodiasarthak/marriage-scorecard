♠ How to Play Marriage

Marriage is a 21-card Rummy game popular in Nepal, India, and Bhutan, played with 3 standard decks (52 + 2/3 Jokers each) and 2-6 players.

Objective

Be the first to arrange your 21 cards into valid sets by completing the Opening and then the Closing contract.

Setup

  • Each player is dealt 21 cards
  • The first player to complete 3 pure sequences picks a random card from the remaining deck, this becomes the Tiplu (Joker). If a printed Joker is drawn, it is treated as A♠
  • Each subsequent player can see the Tiplu only once they complete their own 3 pure sequences
  • The card one rank above Tiplu (same suit) is the Poplu; one rank below is the Jhiplu
  • Cards of the same rank in all other suits also act as wild cards

Valid Sets

  • Pure Sequence 3+ consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g. 4♥ 5♥ 6♥)
  • Sequence 3+ consecutive cards of the same suit using wild cards
  • Tunnel One card of the same rank and suit from each deck (e.g. three 7♣)
  • Dublee A pair of identical cards (e.g. two 5♦) * †
  • Trial Three cards of the same rank (e.g. three 7s) *

* Not valid

Dublee Rules †

  • 7 Dublees: free hand and you may see the Joker
  • 9 Dublees: Show
  • You must commit to one route: once you see the Joker via sequences you cannot play Dublees, and vice versa

Gameplay

  • On your turn, draw one card from the stock pile or the top of the discard pile
  • Then discard one card to end your turn
  • Play passes to the right

Winning a Round (Show)

  • Opening Contract: complete 3 pure sequences (no wild cards)
  • Closing Contract: complete 4 more valid sets after the opening
  • Once the closing contract is met, declare Show, lay down all cards and discard your last card
  • Other players tally their Maal and Hand based on remaining cards

Scoring

  • Maal: points earned from special cards and combinations
  • Hand: penalty based on unmatched cards remaining
  • Show player: own Maal × number of players − Total Maal + Total Hand
  • All others: own Maal × number of players − Total Maal − own Hand

Maal Values

  • Tiplu: 4 Maal; two give 10 Maal
  • Poplu or Jhiplu: 2 Maal each; two of either give 5 Maal (one of each is 4 Maal, not 5); three give 10 Maal
  • Marriage (Tiplu + Poplu + Jhiplu): 10 Maal
  • Tunnel: 2 Maal; two give 5 Maal, three give 10 Maal
  • Joker card: 2 Maal; two give 5 Maal, three give 10 Maal

Hand Values

  • If you have not completed the Opening Contract: Hand = 10 ‡
  • Otherwise, count your unmatched cards: Ace, King, Queen and Jack are worth 10; all other cards are worth their face value
  • Divide the total by 10 and round, your Hand cannot exceed 10

‡ If you have pure sequences longer than 3 cards, you may exclude those cards along with any wild cards and count the rest manually, worth doing if it brings your Hand below 10
